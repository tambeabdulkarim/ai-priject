# Phoenix Content Library — Videos

**⚠️ Verification notice:** the channels, series, and organizations below are real and well-known with high confidence. **Specific video URLs/IDs are deliberately NOT fabricated** — individual video links change, get reuploaded, or get taken down, and guessing a specific URL would be a false claim of verification this document doesn't have. Every lesson that needs a specific video (see `lessons.md`'s template) must have its exact link selected and live-checked during content production — tracked in `content-roadmap.md`. What's provided here is the **trusted source to pull from**, per category, only ever official channels, universities, or well-established conference/creator sources — never re-uploads or unofficial mirrors.

**Legend:** 🟢 High confidence (well-established, verifiable channel/organization) · 🟡 Moderate confidence (real, but less centralized — verify current relevance at production time)

## Duplication Check

## What Was Deliberately NOT Done

No specific video URL or video ID was invented. No claim is made that any specific video currently exists at a specific link — only that the named channel/organization is a real, trusted, official or well-established source to select specific videos from during content production, with each final selection live-verified before publishing (see `quality-standards.md`).
