Executive Summary

The public homepage is a polished, custom-built AI-SaaS marketing page. Everything behind the login wall is a visually different, much plainer product — flat cards, no gradients, no glass, no status color-coding. That gap, plus three reproducible engineering bugs (a header that never shows logged-in state, a broken /instructor/courses route, and a tablet-width header overflow), are the platform's biggest open items. Full reasoning in platform-pixel-audit.md.

Estimated Remaining Work

Unify the logged-in product's card/panel/gradient language with the homepage; add status color-coding across course/order states; fix the header auth-state bug and the tablet breakpoint; repair or remove the /instructor/courses route; clean E2E test data out of admin-facing views before any further design review.

Recommendation

Ship-blocking: the header auth-state bug and the /instructor/courses 404 are functional defects, not polish — fix before any visual work.

Design-blocking: the logged-in product needs its own pass to inherit the homepage's card, gradient, and glass language before this reads as one product.

Not blocking: icon/illustration polish, animation, and skeleton states are real gaps but lowest priority relative to the above.
